First venture

GetTaska

GetTaska is our first venture. It is an artisan marketplace for Nigeria: a mobile-first app that helps people who need work done find skilled artisans near them, from electricians and plumbers to makeup artists and cleaners.

Visit GetTaska

Inside GetTaska

What GetTaska does

Finding a good artisan in Nigeria usually means asking around and hoping. GetTaska turns that into a marketplace, built around the trades people actually need.

The trades people need

Electricians, plumbers, carpenters, AC and generator technicians, painters, tilers, welders, cleaners, and beauty pros, all searchable by the people who need them.

Both sides of the job

One app, two doors. Customers join to find services. Artisans join to be found and to win work.

Near you, on your phone

Built mobile first for Nigeria, and installable straight from the browser with nothing to download from a store.
