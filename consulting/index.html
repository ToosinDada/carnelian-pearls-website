Consulting

I'm Tosin Dada. I help organisations get ServiceNow right the first time.

I'm a two-time ServiceNow MVP, a solution consultant and a lead business analyst. For seven years I've turned messy processes and unclear policy into platform designs that teams can build, sign off and actually use, across ITSM, CSM, ITOM, HRSD and SPM.

I'm based in Lagos, Nigeria, and work remotely with clients worldwide. Lagos is on GMT+1, which overlaps fully with the UK working day and most of the Gulf's, and my recent programmes for clients in the Middle East have been delivered remotely.

I work through CarnelianPearls Technologies Limited, the company I founded, and take engagements directly or on a corp-to-corp basis with clients and delivery partners in Nigeria, the UK, the US, the Middle East and Africa.

What I do

Where I'm most useful

Solution design and platform fit

Shaping scalable ServiceNow designs, making clear out-of-box versus custom decisions, and setting integration strategy and CSDM alignment before build starts.

Discovery and requirements leadership

Running stakeholder workshops, As-Is and To-Be design, and turning the results into a governed requirements baseline, traceable user stories and a build-ready backlog.

Rescue and stabilisation

Taking over escalated or stalled workstreams, restoring backlog quality, and steering requirements back toward standard platform capability.

Service catalogue and process redesign

Rationalising legacy catalogues and approval paths into fewer, clearer, supportable services with sound fulfilment, SLAs and foundation data.

AI in delivery

Putting human-in-the-loop quality gates around AI-generated delivery artefacts, so Now Assist and agentic tools speed up delivery without lowering standards.

Enablement and adoption

Train-the-trainer programmes, end-user training, UAT and hypercare, so what gets configured is what people actually use.

Programmes I've worked on

70%faster mean time to resolve

Energy group, multi-entity rollout

Led requirements and process design for an ITSM and ITOM rollout across many subsidiaries and thousands of users, and trained administrators and end users. The programme reported around a 70% improvement in mean time to resolve.

Public-sector service transformation

Worked on the ITSM and CSM track of a large service-delivery transformation, simplifying a sprawling legacy service catalogue into a smaller set of supportable services.

40%shorter approval cycle

Government HR service delivery
